# Code of conduct

Stip is about looking after yourself. Please look after each other here too.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that people will disagree with you, and that the maintainer makes the final call on what goes into Stip.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, including in memes contributed to the app.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after you've been asked to stop.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@AmreshVs](https://github.com/AmreshVs), through GitHub. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who don't follow this code.

This code applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any other space run for Stip. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
