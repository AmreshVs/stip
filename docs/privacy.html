stip

Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Stip is a desktop app that reminds you to stand up and drink water. It doesn't collect, send or sell any personal data.

What Stip collects

Nothing. Stip has no account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no tracking. The app makes no network requests at all.

What Stip stores on your computer

Stip saves two small files in its own app data folder, so it can remember your choices between launches:

  • Settings: how often to remind you, break length, theme, working hours and similar choices.
  • Stats: how many breaks you took, skipped or snoozed, and how many glasses of water you logged, by day.

These files never leave your computer. Uninstalling Stip, or deleting its app data folder, removes them.

Call detection

To avoid interrupting a video call, Stip checks the names of running programs on your computer for a sign that a call is live, such as Zoom's call process. This check happens on your computer only. Stip doesn't record or send the list of programs, and it never accesses your camera, microphone or screen contents.

The website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, GitHub and Google may log basic request details such as your IP address. The site itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Children

Stip collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Stip is open source, so you can check what it does in the source code.
